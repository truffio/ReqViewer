# Stakeholders and needs

## Stakeholders

| ID | Stakeholder | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SH-01 | Home user | Makes a few coffees a day and cleans the machine weekly. |
| SH-02 | Service technician | Repairs and descales the machine. |

## Needs

| ID | Stakeholder | Need | Meaning for the machine |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| N-001 | SH-01 | "I want a fresh espresso without grinding by hand." | Beans in, cup out. |
| N-002 | SH-01 | "I want to choose how strong it is." | A strength setting. |
| N-003 | SH-01 | "I do not want to get burnt." | Hot water stays inside the machine. |
| N-004 | SH-02 | "I want to replace the heater without special tools." | Serviceable heater. |
